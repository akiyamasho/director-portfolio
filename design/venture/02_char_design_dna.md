# Character Design / DNA: Venture Protagonist

## Design read

-   Story function made visible: a polished young professional who belongs in Venture's contemporary Tokyo workplace world.
-   Apparent age and social coding: mid-to-late 20s, styled with understated confidence and clearly adult proportions rather than teenage school coding.
-   Core contradiction in the design: unresolved in the source material, so do not encode a new one.

## Silhouette and body language

-   Gender presentation / age: feminine young adult.
-   Height / build / proportions: average height, slim natural build, fuller adult bust, mature proportions.
-   Posture: relaxed upright stance with settled shoulders; no exaggerated model pose.
-   Habitual gesture: lightly gathering a few loose strands of hair, as shown in the established visual.
-   Front / side / back silhouette locks: shoulder-length rounded hair mass with longer front locks; loose off-shoulder blouse above a high waist; short flared skirt; slim ankle-strap heels.

## Face, hair, and styling logic

-   Face shape / jaw / cheeks: longer soft oval face, small tapered chin, restrained cheek volume, clearly mature rather than cute.
-   Eyes / iris / highlights: almond-shaped deep blue-violet irises, dark upper lash line, one primary white highlight and one restrained secondary reflection.
-   Brows / nose / mouth: fine brown brows; minimal anime nose; small composed mouth with subtle expression range.
-   Hair mass / part / bangs / side locks / rear silhouette: warm medium brown; side part; diagonal fringe crossing the forehead; one long lock passing the cheek; grouped shoulder-length clumps with a softly rounded back silhouette and a few wind-responsive ends.
-   Skin tone and age markers: warm light-medium skin, smooth young-adult treatment.
-   Grooming: neat and intentional, with natural movement rather than glossy salon rendering.

## Wardrobe and accessories

-   Top / layers: loose matte white blouse with a low, wide off-shoulder wrap neckline, soft gathered folds, and consistent tasteful cleavage.
-   Bottom: dusty-rose high-waisted short flared skirt, tied sash or bow at the waist.
-   Footwear: charcoal pointed closed-toe heels with slim crossing ankle straps.
-   Accessories / props: none established.
-   Forbidden variants: no high or closed neckline, reduced bust, childish face, necklace, glasses, school uniform, turtleneck, burgundy hair, red eyes, galaxy-eye effects, or extra accessories.

## Palette

-   Hair: warm chestnut brown, #9a735f.
-   Eyes: deep blue-violet, #344a91.
-   Skin: warm light-medium peach, #e7b98f.
-   Garment colors: warm white #f5f1e9; dusty rose #c9657b; charcoal #34363b.
-   Accent: muted projector blue #7aa7b8 only in presentation materials, not the costume.

## Personality-specific expression and acting set

1. Neutral public mask: level gaze, relaxed brows, closed mouth, upright head.
2. Socially engaged: attentive eyes and a small polite smile.
3. Private ease or warmth: softened gaze and a quiet genuine smile.
4. Under pressure: slightly drawn brows and held breath, without melodrama.
5. Deflection, control, or attack: sidelong glance with a firm closed mouth.
6. Vulnerability, exhaustion, or consequence: lowered gaze and subtly released posture.

## Continuity and production notes

-   Must survive every angle: side part, diagonal fringe, blue-violet eyes, shoulder-length back mass, blouse neckline, sash construction, heel straps.
-   Scene-specific variants: wind may lift the face-framing locks, but their roots and lengths remain fixed.
-   Comparison with existing cast: distinct from Liz through brown hair, blue-violet eyes, wide blouse neckline, dusty-rose skirt, and lack of jewelry.
-   Unresolved decision: character name and all story-specific behavioral facts.

## House style lock

Refined Hyouka TV-anime / Kyoto Animation naturalism. Thin polished softened-dark linework; clean flat natural colors; one disciplined cel-shadow layer with only occasional restrained secondary shadow; neutral even lighting; grouped hair clumps; detailed but restrained eyes; naturalistic age-appropriate proportions; no bloom, rim light, glossy 3D rendering, painterly texture, photorealism, or cinematic post-processing.
